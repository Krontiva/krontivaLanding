Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Krontiva, KronGage, and KronFlow. Can't find your answer? Get in touch with our team.

General
01What is Krontiva Africa?

Products
05What is KronGage?
+
KronGage is a Customer Engagement Platform built for modern enterprises. It is a collection of loosely coupled SaaS tools sharing a single identity, a single data layer, and a single operational backbone — enabling businesses to engage customers across every channel from one unified platform.
06What is KronFlow?
+
KronFlow is a payment workflow orchestration platform that combines visual workflow design, durable execution, financial message processing, and human workflow management into a single integrated solution. It is purpose-built for financial institutions, fintechs, and enterprises processing high volumes of transactions.
07Are KronGage and KronFlow available as standalone products?
+
Yes. Both KronGage and KronFlow are available as standalone products. They can also be deployed together for organisations that need both customer engagement and payment workflow capabilities under a unified Krontiva infrastructure.
08Is there a free trial available?
+
We offer tailored proof-of-concept engagements for qualified organisations. Contact our team to discuss a pilot programme that fits your business needs and timeline.

Technical
12How does Krontiva handle data security?
+
We employ an industry-leading security stack including data encryption at rest and in transit, strict internal access controls, multi-factor authentication, and a rapid breach notification protocol in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Ghana and applicable international standards.
13What integrations does KronFlow support?
+
KronFlow is built for interoperability and supports integration with major payment rails, core banking systems, mobile money platforms, and ERP/CRM solutions. Our team works with each client to configure the precise integrations required for their existing infrastructure.
14Is KronGage available on cloud and on-premise?
+
Yes. KronGage supports cloud-hosted (SaaS), private cloud, and on-premise deployment models. We work with clients to select the deployment approach that best satisfies their data residency, compliance, and operational requirements.
15What uptime SLA does Krontiva commit to?
+
Our enterprise SLAs include 99.9% platform uptime commitments, 24/7 monitoring, defined incident response windows, and dedicated support channels. Specific SLA terms are negotiated as part of each client agreement.
