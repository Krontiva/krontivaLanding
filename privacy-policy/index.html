We care about your privacy.

Effective Date: March 26, 2026. Krontiva Africa Limited is committed to the highest standards of data sovereignty and privacy.

01.

Introduction

×

Krontiva Africa Limited ("Krontiva," "we," "us," or "our"), a premier business and technology consulting firm headquartered in Accra, Ghana, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our protocols for the collection, orchestration, and protection of your personal identifiers in alignment with global best practices and the Data Protection Act of Ghana.

02.

Data Acquisition

+

We collect information necessary to deliver high-performance transformation services:

  • Identity & Contact: Legal name, professional email, and phone details.
  • Professional Context: Organisational affiliation and industry sector.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP address, device specifications, and interaction metadata.
  • Geolocation: Precise coordinates derived from device settings, processed only with explicit consent.
03.

Strategic Use of Information

+

Your data powers our core service delivery and innovation cycles:

We use your information for operational execution, product evolution (including KronFlow and KronGage), and security monitoring. With your consent, we may also provide personalised engagement through technical insights and promotional updates.

04.

Controlled Disclosure

+

Krontiva does not monetise your personal data. Disclosure is strictly limited to corporate affiliates, vetted third-party service providers, and statutory requirements mandated by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

05.

Security Architecture

+

We employ an industry-leading security stack, including data encryption at rest and in transit, strict internal access controls, and a rapid breach notification protocol in accordance with applicable laws.

06.

Your Individual Rights

+

Under Ghanaian and regional privacy laws, you maintain the following rights:

  • Access & Rectification: Request a transcript or mandate corrections to your data.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion from our active systems.
  • Portability: Request the secure transfer of your data to another provider.
  • Objection: Object to specific processing or marketing activities.
